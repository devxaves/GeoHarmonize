Overview

GeoSync ingests land data in many formats — drone orthorectified imagery (ORI), legacy cadastral maps, revenue records, municipal GIS layers and GNSS survey points — and consolidates them into a single, confidence-scored parcel database. Every automated decision is explainable and reversible, and every change is recorded in an append-only PostGIS audit log.

Confidence scoring

FactorWeightMethod
Geometry overlap35%Intersection-over-union of candidate and baseline boundaries
Owner name match20%RapidFuzz token similarity on recorded owner names
Identifier match15%Survey number / property ID agreement
Source reliability15%Fixed weight per source type
Recency15%More recent surveys score higher

Scores of 90% and above are linked automatically; 60–89% are queued for a reviewer; below 60% remain unresolved pending field verification.
